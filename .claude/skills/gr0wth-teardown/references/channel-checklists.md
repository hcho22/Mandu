# Reading checklists, by channel

Read each top-quarter image and title, and five from the bottom half, against its channel's list.
What the image or text shows is SEEN; why it might work is INFERRED.
Count every trait across the top quarter and the bottom half before calling it a pattern.

## App Store listing

- Title and subtitle: which words are keywords, which are the promise, and how the subtitle changed over time (`data/appstore-history.csv`).
- Screenshots, in order: the headline on each (word count, first-person or second-person, a number or a time), whether the first two carry the whole promise, device frame or full bleed, real UI or a composed scene, people or product, the background color and its hex.
- Which screenshot shows the paywall, the plan, or the result, and how early.
- The first three lines of the description, which show before "more".
- In-app purchase names and prices: the tier ladder and the anchor price.
- Version cadence and what the notes announce (features, fixes, seasonal events).

## YouTube

- Title: length, a number or money, first person ("I built"), a question, brackets, the promise.
- Thumbnail: a face and its emotion, words on it (count them; four or fewer is usual), the product or the outcome shown, a before and after split, contrast and the main color, the logo.
- Whether the title and thumbnail repeat each other or add to each other.
- Shorts against long videos, and length.

## TikTok

- Cover: the frame chosen and the words on it.
- Hook: the first line of the caption and the on-screen text of the opening.
- Format: talking head, screen recording, slideshow, skit, green screen, creator duet or stitch.
- Sound: trending or original, length, hashtag count, posting rhythm.

## Instagram

- The mix of reels, carousels, and photos, and which kind wins.
- Cover or first slide: headline size, word count, template.
- Carousels: slide count and what the last slide asks.
- Caption: the line before "more".
- The grid as a whole: repeated colors, fonts, frames, and where the logo sits.

## X

- The first line before "Show more", length, thread or single post.
- Media: none, a product screenshot, a chart or metric card, a video, a meme.
- Founder account against brand account: which outperforms, and whether numbers (revenue, users) drive the best posts.

## LinkedIn

- The two lines before "see more".
- Format: text, image, document carousel, video.
- People or product in the image.
- Company-page posts against staff posts, and which ones step 5's LinkedIn ads promote.

## Reddit

- Title pattern: a question, a story, "I made".
- Subreddits, self or link post, and whether the account says who it works for.

## Google ads (from `data/ads-google.csv`)

- Format mix (image, video, text) and how long each ran: 60 days or more marks a winner.
- For image creatives in `img/ads-google/`: the headline, the offer (a free trial length, a price, a discount), the person shown, and the call to action.
- The advertiser name on the creatives: the company itself, a holding company, or an agency.

## Bluesky

- As X: the first line, media, and whether the account posts as a person or a brand.
