# Waitlist CTA

## What to stand up (lowest-friction first)

You do not need a landing page to start capturing demand today. Ranked by
time-to-live:

1. **Tally or Google Form** (15 min) — a 4-field form, link it from the post.
   Zero hosting. Good enough to validate; swap later.
2. **Email + ConvertKit/Buttondown** (~30 min) — a hosted signup form with a
   confirmation email. Better if you want to actually nurture the list.
3. **One-page site** (later) — only once you have ~20 signups and a reason to
   invest. Do not block on this.

Recommendation: ship the Tally form today, put the link in post #1, and
upgrade only if signups justify it. The goal this quarter is ~50 signups and
10 interviews, not a polished funnel.

## Form fields (keep it to four)

| Field | Why it earns its place |
|---|---|
| Email | The list. Required. |
| "What do you build, and for whom?" (short text) | Confirms the buyer is actually an agency/freelance dev shipping client AI, not a tire-kicker. |
| "Have you shipped a client RAG/AI feature that needed per-user access?" (yes / no / about to) | Segments warm buyers from curious onlookers; the "yes" and "about to" are your interview list. |
| "Open to a 20-min call about it?" (checkbox) | Self-selects your 10 interview targets straight out of the funnel. |

Do not ask for name, company size, budget, or role title yet. Every extra
field costs signups, and you can ask the rest on the call.

> hit (or are about to), I am packaging the fix, the data model, and the

**Variant C — interview-first (use if you care more about calls than list size):**

> I am validating this with real buyers before I build more of it. If you
> sell client AI features and have 20 minutes, I would rather talk to you
> than guess. Join the waitlist and check the call box: **[waitlist link]**

## Tracking

Once live, log signups + interview-yes counts in the Agentic RAG daily log
under a `## Validation` section so the gate decision has real numbers behind
it. Target checkpoints: 10 signups (signal), 50 signups (quarter goal),
10 completed interviews (the gate input).
