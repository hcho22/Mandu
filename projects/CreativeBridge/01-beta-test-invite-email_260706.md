# Beta-test invite email (friends & family)

**Subject:** Want to test my app before it hits the App Store?

Hi everyone,

Exciting news: I built an app and it's almost ready for the App Store.
Before it goes live, I'd love your help putting it through its paces.

It's called **CreativeBridge**, a story-writing app for kids (K-12) where you build a story together with an AI, one round at a time, and can even turn it into illustrated art.
It's been a blast to make, and now I want real people using it.

If you have an iPhone or iPad, testing takes just a few minutes: you'll install Apple's free **TestFlight** app, open CreativeBridge, write a story or two, and tell me what you think.
What did you love, what felt clunky, did anything break?
If you have kids, even better, since they're exactly who I built it for.

To get you in, just reply with a yes (and the email tied to your Apple ID) and I'll send your invite.

Thank you.
Honest feedback now makes it better for everyone later.

Talk soon,
Hyung

---

## Notes

- ~185 words. Honors the no-emoji / no-em-dash / no-fluff house rules.
- **Delivery options:** the "reply with your Apple ID email" line lets you add testers individually in App Store Connect. To lower friction, swap it for a public TestFlight link ("reply and I'll send you a link to tap") - no email collection needed.
- **To make it more personal:** drop in a one-line anecdote (e.g. a story a niece/nephew made) - beats any generic copy.
- **Shorter variant:** cut the "what did you love / clunky / break" line and the sign-off to get under 130 words.
