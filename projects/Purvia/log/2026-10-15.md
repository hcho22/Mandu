## Record corrected

- **The 2026-10-12 log named the wrong package as the cause of the RAGAS crash.**
  It said the unpinned `datasets` moving from 5.0.1 to 5.1.0 pulled in `pyarrow` 26.
  PyPI shows `datasets` 5.0.1 requires `pyarrow>=21.0.0` and 5.1.0 requires `pyarrow>=24.0.0`, neither with an upper bound, and `pyarrow` 26.0.0 was released on 2026-10-09 at 08:13 UTC, after the 10-04 run (which installed `pyarrow` 25.0.1).
  So the trigger was the `pyarrow` 26.0.0 release itself, and pip would choose it even with `datasets==5.0.1`.
  **The fix has to cap `pyarrow` (for example `pyarrow<26`) in the RAGAS requirements, or move the backend off `numpy<2`; pinning `datasets` alone would not stop the crash.**
  The 10-12 assessment's advice to pin "`datasets` and `pyarrow`" is still right, but only because it includes `pyarrow`.
