Reasons not to use Purvia

  • You need owner / editor / viewer tiers — every grant is read access
  • You need nested groups — flat groups only in v0
  • You need proven recall at 100k+ visible chunks — instrumented, unmeasured
  • You want to search your Slack / Drive — we have no connectors
  • You want a hosted service — Purvia is a codebase you run
all limits documented in docs/permissions-aware-rag.md §6
