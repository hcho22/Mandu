## Notes / Learnings
- Leaky target-mean encoding can produce perfect ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, and F1 because the test labels indirectly leak into the features.
- In-fold target-mean encoding removes the answer-key leak and gives a realistic model comparison.
- On rare fraud data, F1 at the default `0.5` threshold is only one operating point. Threshold tuning, PR-AUC, and precision/recall tradeoffs matter more.
