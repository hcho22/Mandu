# T-Shaped Portfolio

The portfolio shape [[ai-era-50-career-tips]] says recruiters currently want: broad breadth across AI capabilities plus at least one deep, foundational area of real depth.

## Key points

- **Breadth** (the top of the T): know what is possible across agentic tools, and jump between domains to orchestrate multiple agents at a high level.
- **Depth** (the stem): one foundational area you have taken to root level, built by picking a direction and sticking with it rather than chasing a new "mountain" each time it gets hard.
- Depth is earned by shipping one thing end-to-end and maintaining it for 1-3 years ("the scars to prove it").
- **Pick a direction to deepen** ([[zero-to-hireable-ai-engineer]]): after the buildable core, choose one stem rather than spreading thin - AI product engineer (startup-fast; polished apps + product judgment), applied ML (fine-tuning, local models, inference optimization), or AI automation (business workflows across email/CRM/docs; n8n, LangGraph). The concrete portfolio target is three deployed projects, each with an honest README.

## A portfolio spec, not just a shape (2026-08-01)

[[fde-in-30-days]] supplies what this page lacked: **a checkpointed specification** for what one deep portfolio piece has to contain, rather than a count of projects.
See [[do-the-job-before-the-title]] for the full four-week version.

The two requirements that most portfolios miss:

- **Document the iteration history, not just the architecture.**
"Accuracy went from 70% to 95%" demonstrates *method*; a finished architecture diagram demonstrates only outcome.
For an evals/quality role that history is the entire artifact.
- **Rehearse the pitch two ways - as an engineer and as a VP.**
This maps directly onto [[two-kinds-of-judgment]], and being unable to give one of the two is the diagnostic for which side of the T is actually shallow.

The entry tactic attached to it is also sharper than "build in public": **do the first engagement free**, prove value, and only charge after 2-3 proven cases.

## Synthesis

This is a direct prescription for hcho's portfolio strategy: Agentic RAG, FitSnack, and CreativeBridge provide breadth across RAG, product, and multi-model apps, while the validation/evals angle can be the deep stem that differentiates him.
The risk the concept warns against - motion mistaken for progress across many unfinished projects - is the exact failure mode a multi-bet portfolio must guard against, which argues for finishing and maintaining, not just starting.
Pairs with [[build-in-public]] (breadth made visible) and [[taste-as-moat]] (depth made credible).
