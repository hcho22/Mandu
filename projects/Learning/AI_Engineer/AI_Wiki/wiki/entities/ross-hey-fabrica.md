# Ross (Hey Fabrica)

Developer and founder of the **Hey Fabrica** agency; builder of **Pluto**, an AI-agent product for businesses (~15-60 employees, manager-invites-employees model).

## What shows up in the wiki

- [[building-robust-apps-with-ai]] - teaches production [[system-design]] through Pluto's real architecture: [[engineering-trade-offs]], [[everything-as-code-abstractions]], [[monorepo-for-agent-context]], [[client-never-touches-the-database]], and [[durable-agent-processes]].
- Opinionated default stack: SvelteKit + Vercel for the front end, [[convex]] as the backend/DB control plane, Effect TS for services, WorkOS for enterprise auth, Autumn for credit billing, OpenClaw ([[open-claw]]) as the deployed agent, Daytona for agent compute.

## Notes

Explicitly frames his picks as informed agency preferences, not universal law ("this isn't the greatest way, it's how I build").
