# Chip Huyen

Engineer and author of *AI Engineering: Building Applications with Foundation Models* and *Designing Machine Learning Systems* (O'Reilly); a foundational, systems-minded voice on building and evaluating AI applications.

## What shows up in the wiki

- [[evaluate-ai-systems]] - Chapter 4 of *AI Engineering*: evaluation criteria, model selection (build-vs-buy, reading benchmarks), and how to design an [[evaluation-pipeline]]. Introduces [[evaluation-criteria]], [[factual-consistency]], [[model-build-vs-buy]], and a rigorous treatment of [[data-contamination]].

## Notes

Alongside [[hamel-husain]] (product-eval), [[eugene-yan]] (systems patterns), and [[clementine-fourrier]] (benchmarking), one of the core eval voices in this wiki; Huyen is the most end-to-end, book-length treatment.
Her "evaluation is the biggest bottleneck to AI adoption" thesis anchors the [[evals]] area.
