# Vas

Founder of **Varick Agents**, an agency deploying AI systems inside enterprises.
The wiki's primary source on the [[forward-deployed-engineer]] role.

Reports working across roughly **50 enterprise clients**, where he observes the same tooling stack repeatedly: [[claude-code]], Cursor, GitHub Copilot.
Previously a software engineer at Meta; dropped out of a computer-science program around 2008-2009 over the gap between the curriculum and the then-new App Store.

## Positions worth knowing

- **Model agnosticism is right for a company and wrong for a beginner.** Varick Agents is deliberately model agnostic, but his advice to an individual is the opposite: **pick one model and one agent platform and get very good at it first.** "Don't put the cart ahead of the horse."
- **Sell the audit; don't give it away** - some clients told him the audit alone was worth 10x what they paid. But for someone breaking in, **do the first one free**.
- **Only 2-3 steps of a 10-step workflow genuinely need LLM judgment.** The rest is if/else and API calls.
- **Full traceability is non-negotiable** for client trust, not just for debugging.
- Skeptical that universities will catch up on AI/agents curricula; points to YouTube and X as the current sources.

## Notes

Read with the incentives visible: he is an agency founder describing why the role his agency sells is valuable and well-paid.
Two figures should be treated as reported rather than verified - the **95% of generative AI pilots fail** MIT statistic (cited second-hand) and the **$150K-$1M** compensation range (his characterization of the market, not survey data).

That said, this is the most **directly career-actionable** source in the wiki for hcho, because the role it describes rewards validation and exception-cataloguing judgment rather than discounting it.
