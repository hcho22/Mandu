# Clémentine Fourrier

Researcher at [[huggingface]] who leads evaluation and leaderboards (Open LLM Leaderboard); a foundational academic-practitioner voice on rigorous LLM evaluation.

## What shows up in the wiki

- [[lets-talk-about-llm-evaluation]] - her taxonomy of the [[three-eval-methods]] (benchmarks / human / model as judge) and the [[purposes-of-evaluation]] (non-regression, ranking, capability), plus a clear treatment of [[data-contamination]].
- [[llm-evaluation-guidebook]] - the exhaustive OpenEvals/HF guidebook she led (with Frere, Penedo, Thomas Wolf): the fullest treatment of contamination + [[benchmark-saturation]], [[score-reproducibility]], [[functional-scorers]], and the LLM-judge bias catalog.

## Notes

Comes at evals from the leaderboard/benchmarking side, which complements the product-eval angle of [[hamel-husain]] and [[eugene-yan]] and the end-to-end book treatment of [[chip-huyen]].
Her recurring stance: prefer interpretable functional testing over model judges, and evaluation is never finished.
