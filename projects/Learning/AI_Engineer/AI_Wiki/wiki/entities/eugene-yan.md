# Eugene Yan

Applied/ML engineer and prolific writer (eugeneyan.com); a foundational practitioner voice on building LLM systems, evals, and RecSys.
Alongside [[hamel-husain]], one of the two most-cited practitioner sources in this wiki's eval and production-LLM material.

## What shows up in the wiki

- [[llm-patterns]] - his landmark "Patterns for Building LLM-based Systems & Products" (2023): the seven-pattern map (evals, RAG, fine-tuning, caching, guardrails, defensive UX, feedback) that opens the [[production-llm]] area and deepens [[evals]] and [[rag]].

## Notes

Writes from hands-on production experience (Obsidian-Copilot, RecSys, search); strong on the "demos are easy, products are hard" thesis.
Expect his other writing (abstractive summarization evals, design patterns) to recur as more sources land.
