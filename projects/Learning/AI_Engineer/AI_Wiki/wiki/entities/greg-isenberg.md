# Greg Isenberg

Founder and host of the **Startup Ideas Podcast**, a widely-followed show on building internet businesses, increasingly focused on how founders and small teams use AI.

## What shows up in the wiki

- [[graph-engineering-isenberg]] - a monologue episode defining [[graph-engineering]] (designing the work around AI as a graph of jobs, arrows, and state) and distinguishing it from [[knowledge-graph]]s. His framing: graph engineering is "the next logical step after prompting," and the people who get the most from AI will be those who break work into the right pieces, give each the right context, check the output, and keep the human in the right place.

## Notes

The Startup Ideas Podcast is the same source lineage as [[agents-are-the-new-saas]] and [[startups-for-agents]] (both filed to it in earlier ingests), so this show is now the wiki's main **founder/operator** voice on agents - a useful counterweight to the engineering-practitioner sources ([[tharic]], [[boris-cherny]], [[kun]]): it names the same structures ([[self-referential-bias]], [[start-as-a-workflow]], [[agent-teams]]) in language aimed at builders without an engineering background.
Isenberg's stance throughout is that most viral AI terms "make everyone feel behind," and that graph engineering is a rare one that names something genuinely useful.
