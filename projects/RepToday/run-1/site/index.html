How it works

Discipline is the product. Everything else serves it.

Assembled before you arrive

A deterministic on-device engine builds today's session from 42 zero-equipment movements: strength, mobility, and primal work, fit to your minutes within one minute of accuracy, in under 100 milliseconds, fully offline. No browsing. No choosing. No question before you start.

Quietly retunes to you

Between sessions, Nonzero rereads your history and adjusts the program: progression pace, variety, how much of each pillar. It tells you what it changed in one honest sentence. It never touches the moment you press Start.

Dents, never zeroes

The Consistency Score rewards showing up. A 5-minute session counts in full. A missed day costs a visible dent that fades. Coming back after a break is served easy and celebrated, never punished. Your longest chain is kept as pride, not held hostage.

Straight answers

The questions you should ask.

Why no streaks? Streaks work.

They do, while they last. Duolingo's own research says a 7-day streak makes you 3.6x more likely to finish your course, and also that breaking one is so demotivating they built freezes, wagers, and amulets to soften it. Every measured retention win they publish is a forgiveness mechanic bolted onto the streak. Nonzero builds the forgiveness in structurally instead of selling it back to you. If you love your chain, we count it. It just can't hurt you here.

Will 5-minute workouts actually do anything?

For cardio fitness, yes: a 2025 meta-analysis of 11 randomized trials found significant improvement in inactive adults, with 82.8% adherence. For visible muscle or fat loss, no, and we won't pretend otherwise. Short sessions build the habit and real cardio fitness; strength programming unlocks as an earned phase, with the volume that goal actually requires.

Is this another AI coach?

No. The workout you see was assembled by a deterministic engine that runs on your phone, offline, in under 100 milliseconds. A quiet programmer layer re-tunes your plan between sessions and tells you what it changed in plain language. Nothing generates your workout on the fly, nothing waits on a server, and nothing is marketed to you as magic.

Who is this NOT for?

People who already train consistently and want optimization: apps like Dr. Muscle and BodBot adapt sets and loads for committed lifters, and they're good at it. Nonzero is for the person who has quit three fitness apps and needs the showing-up problem solved first. If browsing workout libraries is part of the fun for you, Nike Training Club is free and excellent.

What happens to my data?

Your history lives on your device and in your private iCloud. No account is required for the core loop. The one optional network call (a line of session language) sends no personal data and falls back to an offline template if you're in airplane mode.
