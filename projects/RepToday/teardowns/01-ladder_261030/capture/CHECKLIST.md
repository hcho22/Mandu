# Capture checklist: Ladder

These sources refuse automated readers or show numbers only in a browser.
Open each address in your own browser, take the screenshots described, and save them under
`capture/<channel>/` as `01.png`, `02.png`, and so on.
Add one row per screenshot to `capture/captures.csv` (file, channel, url, captured_at, notes).
Skip anything you do not want to do; the report then says that channel was not captured.
Nothing here needs an account, a purchase, or a message to anyone.

## 1. Tiktok: joinladder

- Open: https://tiktok.com/@joinladder
- Capture: Open the profile, wait for the grid, and screenshot it so every cover's play count shows; scroll and screenshot again until about 60 videos or 6 months are covered. Then open the 'Popular' tab and take one more screenshot.
- Save to: `capture/tiktok/`

## 2. Instagram: joinladder

- Open: https://www.instagram.com/joinladder/reels/
- Capture: Open the Reels tab (it shows play counts) and screenshot until about 60 reels are covered; then screenshot the main grid once for the house style. For a carousel that looks like a winner, open it and screenshot the first and last slide.
- Save to: `capture/instagram/`

## 3. X: joinladder

- Open: https://twitter.com/joinladder
- Capture: Open the profile and screenshot posts top to bottom so each one's text, views, and date show; about 60 posts or 6 months. Do the same for each founder account listed.
- Save to: `capture/x/`

## 4. Linkedin: company/joinladder

- Open: https://www.linkedin.com/company/joinladder/posts/
- Capture: Open the company page's Posts tab and screenshot about 20 posts with their reactions; note any post that says 'Promoted'.
- Save to: `capture/linkedin/`

## 5. App Store search ranks

- Open: the App Store app on your iPhone (Apple refuses automated App Store search).
- Capture: search each term below and screenshot the first screen of results.
- Terms: strength training; workout plan; home workout; gym workout; fitness coach; workout app for women; quick workout; micro workout; 10 minute workout; no equipment workout
- Save to: `capture/appstore-search/`

## 6. Reddit: posts that name Ladder

- Open: https://www.reddit.com/search/?q=Ladder&sort=top&t=year
- Capture: Open the search (it is sorted by top posts of the past year) and screenshot the first two screens; open the top 3 threads and save each one's address in captures.csv.
- Save to: `capture/reddit/`

## 7. Meta Ad Library (Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, Audience Network)

- Open: https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=US&q=Ladder&search_type=keyword_unordered&media_type=all
- Capture: the result count, then screenshot the ads in view (each shows its start date); for Meta, sort or scan for the oldest ads still active, which are the long-running winners.
- Save to: `capture/meta/`

## 8. TikTok Ad Library (ads shown in the EU and UK)

- Open: https://library.tiktok.com/ads?region=all&adv_name=Ladder&sort_type=last_shown_date,desc
- Capture: the result count, then screenshot the ads in view (each shows its start date); for Meta, sort or scan for the oldest ads still active, which are the long-running winners.
- Save to: `capture/tiktok-ads/`

## 9. LinkedIn Ad Library

- Open: https://www.linkedin.com/ad-library/search?accountOwner=Ladder
- Capture: the result count, then screenshot the ads in view (each shows its start date); for Meta, sort or scan for the oldest ads still active, which are the long-running winners.
- Save to: `capture/linkedin-ads/`

## 10. Apple Ad Repository (App Store ads shown in the EU; search the developer name)

- Open: https://adrepository.apple.com/
- Capture: the result count, then screenshot the ads in view (each shows its start date); for Meta, sort or scan for the oldest ads still active, which are the long-running winners.
- Save to: `capture/apple-ads/`

## 11. Google Trends: search interest in Ladder

- Open: https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=today+5-y&geo=US&q=Ladder
- Capture: click the download arrow on 'Interest over time' and save `multiTimeline.csv` into `capture/trends/`. It is the free stand-in for paid traffic history.
- Save to: `capture/trends/multiTimeline.csv`
