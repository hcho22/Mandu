### Strength Phase system (US-SP01-SP06, PRs #136-#141, 2026-08-26)

The Strength Phase was previously **inert in production** - a known open defect this tracker had flagged. This wave builds it out, and US-SP01 closes the defect directly.

- **US-SP01** (#136, `767307c`) - **lift the difficulty cap when Strength Phase is earned.** This is the defect fix. Verified from the PR: a phase-gated skill had to clear both `ExercisePoolFilter.isPhaseAllowed` *and* a difficulty cap derived purely from the self-reported `FitnessLevel` (beginner 1-2, intermediate 1-3, advanced 1-5). Every `.strength` skill in the catalog is difficulty 5, so a beginner/intermediate user who *earned* the Strength Phase still saw nothing - the "double-gate trap." #136 adds a centralized `effectiveDifficultyCap(for level:phase:)` that lifts the cap to the full `1...5` range for a user whose earned `phase == .strength`, keying off `user.phase` (PhaseEvaluator's earned output), never a re-derivation. The PR's own real-catalog evidence confirms the fix: for one intermediate user, `push_one_arm` (d5, strength-gated) is unreachable in `.discipline` and reachable after PhaseEvaluator earns `.strength`. `.discipline` behavior is byte-identical (pinned by test). Full unit gate 1062 tests, 0 failures.
- **US-SP02** (#137, `f192bcc`) - mid-tier phase-gated skills for push/squat/core (so there is a real climb to reach, not just d5 cliffs).
- **US-SP03** (#138, `fd9d616`) - a missing hinge strength ladder in the catalog.
- **US-SP04** (#139, `22fd7c2`) - a phase-progress surface, "the visible climb."
- **US-SP05** (#140, `c08d536`) - a progression-map ladder tied to Strength Phase.
- **US-SP06** (#141, `824b18c`) - the Strength-Phase graduation moment.

So the "Strength Phase is inert" gap is closed: US-SP01 makes earning it actually change the workout, and US-SP02-SP03 give it somewhere to climb, with US-SP04-SP06 surfacing the climb and the graduation.

### AI Coach premium feature (US-AC01-AC08, PRs #142-#149, 2026-08-26 → 08-27)

This is a genuinely new **LLM surface** - the app's second, after the Variety Language slice. Verified in the code: `CoachProxyClient` (`ios/RepToday/RepToday/Services/Coach/`) POSTs a non-identifying `CoachContextBundle` to a `/coach` route on a stateless proxy (`proxy/src/worker.js`) and returns **Claude's reply**; the Anthropic API key is held server-side, and the bundle carries no `installId`, no IDFA. It mirrors `ProxyVarietyLanguageProvider`'s contract.

- **US-AC01** (#142, `0e7e12e`) - a **stateless coach data boundary** (the audited `CoachContextBundle`; nothing identifying leaves the device).
- **US-AC02** (#143, `3d9fde6`) - the **"talking coach" chat surface**.
- **US-AC03** (#144, `c5d66fb`) - **premium-gate the coach with a free upsell entry point**.
- **US-AC04** (#145, `221d266`) - **pre-use data disclosure with a send-path consent gate** (the user sees what would be sent, and consents, before any request leaves).
- **US-AC05** (#146, `3933396`) - a **bounded pattern-emphasis policy lever** the coach can nudge.
- **US-AC06** (#147, `7a2e5fa`) - **asymmetric pace easing**: a coach can only ease pace *down*, never up (safety rail).
- **US-AC07** (#148, `523811c`) - a **bounded two-writer policy write path**: a coach-sourced write is stamped `updatedBy == .llm` and clamped disjoint/only-downward through the one sanctioned `SessionPolicy` producer, so the LLM can shape policy but never bypass the deterministic guardrails.
- **US-AC08** (#149, `c9e0424`) - **injury-signal routing to an explicit, reversible flag** rather than silent behavior change.

The design pattern here is consistent and worth noting for the AI-builder story: an LLM that writes *policy through a clamped, audited, reversible seam*, never the workout - the same "AI writes policy, never the session" discipline the deterministic engine already enforces, now extended to a conversational surface.

## Notes / Learnings

- **Strategic tension worth surfacing (a captain call, not resolved here).** The README's own thesis says the objective for this cycle is **"not more product - ship and find out whether there is PMF."** Yet 2026-08-26 → 08-28 shipped a substantial *new* premium feature set: a Claude-backed AI Coach (8 stories), a built-out Strength Phase (6 stories), and premium analytics (2 stories). Two readings, and I am deliberately leaving the judgment open:
  - **Deliberate premium-monetization build:** the paywall gates *depth*, and Strength Phase + AI Coach + deep analytics are exactly the depth layer the monetization plan names as "as they land." Building the premium tier out before launch means the freemium upsell is real on day one, not a promise. The AI Coach also materially strengthens the AI-builder portfolio story.
  - **Scope creep against ship-first sequencing:** none of this was a submission blocker, and the actual gates to the PMF test (below) are provisioning and assets, which did not move. Three days of feature engineering is three days not spent capturing the real-device benchmark or provisioning Convex. The Strength Phase, by the app's own product model, is *earned* and no launch-cohort user will have earned it, so at launch none of the SP work is even reachable by a new user.
  - Both can be true. Flagging it the way prior entries flag such tensions; the sequencing call belongs above this log.
- **The AI Coach is a real second LLM call, not a deterministic feature.** That is a meaningful change to the app's shape and its App Store / privacy story: a premium chat surface that sends a (non-identifying, consented) context bundle to Anthropic via the proxy. The consent gate (#145) and stateless boundary (#142) are built to keep the shipped privacy claims true, but the nutrition-label and privacy-policy story should be re-checked against this surface before submission.
