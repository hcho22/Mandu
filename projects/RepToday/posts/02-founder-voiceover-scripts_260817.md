# Rep Today - Founder voice-over scripts (P02, P07, P09, P10)

**Created:** 2026-08-17
**For:** the four founder-first-person angles in the 14-day PMF cadence (`gtm/05-social-pmf/`).
**Format:** voice-over + faceless visuals (hcho's real recorded voice over screen recordings and b-roll), replacing the kit's default "founder-on-camera."
See the decision logged 2026-08-17 in `decisions/log.md` and the note in `03-social-media-execution-checklist_260817.md`.

## Rules that bind every script (from `angles.md` / `positioning.md`)

- The **hook line is frozen** - deliver it verbatim; only the surrounding body is adjustable.
- Real recorded human voice only. **Never** the macOS `say` TTS or any AI voice (the exact thing the on-camera rule existed to prevent).
- Mechanics-only: describe our own behavior, never a competitor's motive. "most" is the ceiling, never "every".
- Identity-framed, never loss-framed. No health or physique claims. No invented numbers the screen does not visibly justify.
- "Rep Today" in prose. The only status claim allowed pre-launch is "iOS, not released yet".
- No em dashes in captions.
- Confirm each demoed behavior actually exists in the build on the phone before filming the insert.

## P02 - Comeback story (Day 4) - target 20-30s

**Visuals:** Screen recording of the return-after-a-gap flow (app opens to an easy session, the comeback acknowledgment). Optionally intercut a 1-2s shot of a wall calendar with visible gaps, or an empty mat.

**Voice-over:**
> **"I skipped four days and my app celebrated my comeback. On purpose. I built it that way."** *(verbatim)*
> Most apps treat a missed day like failure. The streak resets, and the guilt does the rest.
> I wanted the opposite. In Rep Today, a gap isn't a reset. You come back, it meets you where you are, and it treats showing up again as the win. Because it is.

**On-screen text:** open with "I skipped 4 days." -> later "my app celebrated it."

**Caption:** `Missed a few days? Good. Come back anyway. Rep Today, a micro-workout app for real life. iOS, not released yet.`

## P07 - Free-forever wedge (Days 2 and 9, same footage, two cuts) - target 20s

**Visuals:** The real 1-star review shown as on-screen text (blur or crop out the app name and the reviewer's handle - a pain pattern, not a named competitor). Then a screen recording scrolling through the free workouts.

### Cut A - pain-first (Day 2)
**Voice-over:**
> Someone left this on a workout app: "you cannot use this app unless you pay for it. You just can't."
> That's the moment people quit.
> **"So in mine, the workouts are free. All of them. Forever."** *(verbatim)*

**On-screen text:** the review quote appears as read -> then "the workouts are free. all of them."

### Cut B - promise-first (Day 9)
**Voice-over:**
> **"So in mine, the workouts are free. All of them. Forever."** *(verbatim)*
> No trial that quietly converts. No wall halfway in.
> I kept seeing reviews like this: "you cannot use this app unless you pay for it. You just can't." That's the app I didn't want to build.

**On-screen text:** "the workouts are free. forever." -> then the review quote appears.

**Caption (both):** `Free workouts. Not a trial, not a tease. Rep Today. iOS, not released yet.`

**Honesty note:** keep "the workouts are free" true to the build - the free core loop is free; premium "depth" is the paid tier. The line is about the workouts themselves, which is accurate. Do not imply the whole app has no paid tier.

## P09 - No-streaks contrarian (Days 6 and 13, same footage, two cuts) - target 20-30s

**Visuals:** Screen recording of the missed-day Consistency Score dipping a little and NOT resetting to zero (the P03 asset).

### Cut A - declaration-led (Day 6)
**Voice-over:**
> **"I built a workout app with no streaks, on purpose. Watch what happens when I miss a day."** *(verbatim)*
> No fire emoji. No "you lost your streak."
> Just a consistency score that dips a little, and never drops to zero. Miss a day, come back, keep going. That's the whole mechanic.

**On-screen text:** "no streaks. on purpose." -> "watch a missed day."

### Cut B - curiosity-led (Day 13)
**Voice-over:**
> What actually happens in a workout app with no streaks when you miss a day?
> **"I built a workout app with no streaks, on purpose. Watch what happens when I miss a day."** *(verbatim)*
> The score dips a little. It never resets. That's it.

**On-screen text:** open on a question: "no streaks. so what happens when you miss?"

**Caption (both):** `No streaks to lose. A missed day dips your score, it never wipes it. Rep Today. iOS, not released yet.`

## P10 - Discipline inversion (Day 12, the ONE sanctioned test) - target 15-20s

**Visuals:** A plain title card for the spoken line (or hands setting the phone down), then cut immediately to the cold-open demo (the P01 asset). No explaining sentence between the line and the demo - the demo is the punchline.

**Voice-over:**
> **"The most disciplined thing I own is a workout app. Watch."** *(verbatim)*
> *(then silence over the cold-open demo - do not narrate over it)*

**On-screen text:** "the most disciplined thing I own" -> *(demo plays)*

**Caption:** `It opens straight to the workout, so I don't have to decide. Rep Today. iOS, not released yet.`

**Delivery note:** say the line dry and a little self-aware, like an observation, never like a command. This angle gets one test only - if the comments read it as bossy / drill-sergeant, it retires permanently, and tone is what decides that.
