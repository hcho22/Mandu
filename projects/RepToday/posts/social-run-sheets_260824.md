## Posting rules (every day, every post)

- One asset/day to **all four surfaces in the same locked evening slot**. Do not stagger across days - the A/B legs post 7 days apart in the same weekday/slot, so the time must be held constant.
- **Only TikTok (primary) and YouTube Shorts (second sample) adjudicate.** Instagram Reels and X are reach mirrors: their numbers are informational only and never kill, promote, or rank an angle.
- A post counts only at **>=200 impressions** on its primary platform; below that it is UNREAD and triggers no decision.
- **Reply to every substantive comment within 24h.** TikTok/Shorts comment language feeds the scored read and next window's hook-mining.
- Pre-pub check: status true (pre-launch, no listing/link/install counts), "Rep Today" in prose with no (R)/registration claim, AI disclosure in-post if any post says "AI", no em dashes, no health claims, no named competitor, real-device/real-time footage with cuts only where declared.
