- **What the app is:** a Swift 6 / SwiftUI app for iOS 26, iPhone only, generated by XcodeGen (`App/Immerse/project.yml`, with the `.xcodeproj` committed).
  The bundle ID is `com.immerse.FilmJournal` and the display name is "Immerse".
  Seven local Swift packages split the domain, raw-SQLite persistence, Trial runtime, rendering, AVFoundation/PhotoKit adapters, StoreKit 2 plus Keychain entitlement, and media catalog.
  Screens cover Journal, Camera catalog, Load Film, capture, Film detail, Photo with Darkroom, Soundtrack, Subscription, and Settings.
  All five v1 Cameras are present (Disposable 27, Instant 10, 6x6 12, Super 8 3:20, 16mm 2:45).
  The media catalog is empty because no licensed samples or music exist yet, and live StoreKit product IDs and prices are deliberately unconfigured.
