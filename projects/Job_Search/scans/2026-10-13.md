# Weekly job search - 2026-10-13

**As of:** 2026-10-13 16:15 PDT (America/Los_Angeles)

**Result:** **17 qualifying full-time openings at Fit 4-5** (one Fit 5, sixteen Fit 4), **20 more held back by one open question**, 4 held by the seniority rule, and **2 contracts, both conditional**.
Eleven of the 17 are new this week and six carry forward from October 5.
For the first time, the AI quality and evaluation lane has roles that clear every gate (four of them).
The strongest new find is Arbiter AI's QA Engineer for voice AI agents: remote, $180K-$200K, posted October 8, with a years bar in QA generally that the Zebra decade speaks to.
No software quality role clears every gate; the closest ones do not post pay or need a browser test framework (Playwright) that the Master profile does not show.

**How this week was scored.**
Every role, new or carried, was scored against the Master profile using the rule from the [2026-10-08 re-score](./2026-10-08-rescore.md).
Paid software time is 2.0 years (C# test automation at Zebra), Omdena is volunteer work, and project work meets a years bar only when the posting does not say "professional".
One rule was added this week because it kept deciding roles: when a posting's lead requirement is production AI used by real users ("not just prototypes"), the current projects do not meet it, and the role is Fit 3.
That rule, applied to last week's queue, is why the list changed more than usual.

No application or proposal was submitted, and no existing status was changed.
