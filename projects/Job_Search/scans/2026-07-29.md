## Structural finding - the most important output of this run

**The remote-US AI-builder market at these companies is far thinner than the raw posting counts suggest, and
the thinness is systematic, not seasonal.** Reading every genuinely-remote-US posting across all 58 boards,
the overwhelming majority are **GTM roles**: Sierra's only 7 remote-US postings are all Sales Director /
Enterprise Sales Leader ($295K-$400K). Cursor's 19 remote postings are almost entirely Strategic Account
Executives. Ramp's 9 are Solutions/Technical Consultants. Harvey's 14 are counsel, support ops, and analysts.
LangChain's 5 are Solutions Architects and an Enterprise AE. **These companies hire engineers onsite in
SF/NYC and hire revenue remotely.**

Deepgram is the standout exception - a genuinely remote-first engineering org with 52 remote-US postings
including a deep engineering bench - which is why it supplies both this run's top pick and several of the
prior run's leads.

**Implication for the 5/week target:** a weekly ATS sweep of this company list will not sustainably produce
5 qualifying applications/week under the tightened gates. This run yielded **3** (1 Fit 5, 2 Fit 4) from a
fully-verified read of 58 boards. That is an honest ceiling, not a scan failure. Options to raise throughput,
in rough order of expected value: (1) **expand the company list** toward remote-first AI companies
specifically (the Deepgram profile) rather than the current SF-centric frontier-lab/evals-vendor list;
(2) **relax the LA radius** to greater LA/SoCal to open onsite roles, which is where the engineering jobs
actually are; (3) **accept a lower weekly number** and compensate with higher per-application quality. Worth
a decision before the next scan.
