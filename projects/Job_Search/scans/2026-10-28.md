## What the market said

- Fresh Upwork AI work clusters at $100-$200 fixed for agent builds; listings priced at $500-$3,000 are rare, and hourly AI work mostly sits at $10-$45.
- Fresh QA automation demand on Upwork is almost entirely Playwright with TypeScript, at 30+ hours a week for 3-6 months or more.
  Playwright is the single skill gap that shut out the most software quality listings this scan.
- No fresh indexed listing asked for Swift, XCTest, React Native AI features, firmware, or hardware validation.
- The listings that paid $40-$120/hour and matched evaluation work were all a month or more old.
