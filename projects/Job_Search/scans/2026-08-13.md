# Job Scan - 2026-08-13

**Method:** live-read of the ATS APIs (Ashby `posting-api` + Greenhouse `boards-api`) via curl, so every row below has a verified `workplaceType`/location and, where the ATS discloses it, a posted comp band. Aggregator summaries were not used.

**Filters applied:** builder roles only (core work = building with LLMs/agents/RAG or shipping AI product features); remote-US-eligible OR within 25 mi of Burbank; Fit 4-5 only. Gate 1 (seniority honestly clearable - no 5+/6+ specialized-spine bars, no founding/lead) and Gate 2 (real comp + real remote-US via `workplaceType`, not the `isRemote` boolean) both enforced.

**Result: 3 verified leads (1 Fit 5, 2 Fit 4).** This is a thin list, and it matches the standing structural finding: at these target companies, genuinely-remote-US postings are overwhelmingly GTM (Sales/AE/Solutions), while engineering is hired onsite in SF/NYC. Deepgram remains the remote-first engineering exception. Per policy I am not padding to hit 5 - see "Dropped / notable" for the near-misses.
