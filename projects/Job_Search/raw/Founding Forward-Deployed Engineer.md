About Closure

Closure helps law enforcement search evidence and solve crime. Public servants across the U.S. use Closure to expedite investigations into Cold Cases, homicides, gang conflicts, sexual assault, and other serious offenses. We’re live with police and prosecutors and actively growing our footprint to reduce evidence overload through the criminal justice system.

Closure is well-financed and backed by reputable Seed investors (YCombinator, CRV, Hawktail, Liquid2, Adverb, Commonweal, and others).

Role Description:

Live With Users: You are thrilled to apply your engineering skills to solving real-world user problems, directly with users (and frequently in-person).

Pre-Scale Problem Solver: You are a strong engineer, but you are comfortable building things that do not “scale”; whether a one-off analysis or an integration.

Product Thinker & Tinkerer: Your experience on the frontlines with customers will directly translate into product roadmaps. You are an executor who turns customer pain into PRs, or clear understandings of what/how we must build.

About You

Mission-Driven: Excited to contribute to U.S. government competence and public safety.

Owner-Builder Mindset: Bias toward action, thrives in extremely fast-paced environments. Looking to do the best work of your life.

Comfort with Ambiguity: We are an early-stage startup with many exciting questions to be answered; you run toward unasked and unanswered questions.
