## Strategy (updated 2026-09-20)

**Primary strategy: three complementary lanes, broad enough to improve opportunity volume while remaining evidence-backed.**

1. **Software quality and systems validation:** SDET; Software QA Automation; Systems Validation; Verification and Validation; Product Quality; Test Infrastructure; API/backend automation; Mobile/iOS Test; and Release Quality. Favor automation, APIs, mobile, connected devices, developer tools, and AI products. Exclude manual-only QA and pure hardware-only validation with no meaningful software or automation ownership.
2. **AI quality and evaluation:** AI Quality; AI Test; AI/ML Validation; LLM or Model Evaluation; AI Reliability; ML Systems QA; Evaluation Platform; Responsible AI Test; and product-evaluation-focused AI red teaming. Validation tenure supplies rigor, but does not substitute for a required multi-year record of shipping software test infrastructure or evaluation systems.
3. **AI product, integration, and technical delivery:** AI/Applied AI/Agentic/LLM/GenAI/Product Engineer; Integration or Implementation Engineer; Customer Engineer; hands-on Technical Solutions Engineer; Developer or Technical Support for APIs, developer tools, or AI platforms; Product Engineer for internal tools/workflow automation; Forward Deployed AI Engineer; and software/full-stack roles centered on shipping AI features.

Preserve qualified AI-builder roles; the broader lanes add credible opportunities rather than replacing them. Apply at the Mid-level and Senior IC levels when the stated experience and core competencies are honestly clearable. Exclude manual QA, annotation, generic support, quota-dominated sales, leadership-heavy Staff/Principal/Lead, research/PhD-gated work, and roles whose spine is an unsupported frontend, SDK/library, DevOps/SRE, MLOps-platform, data-science, or advanced-ML specialty.

Use an approximate **40/35/25 application mix** across the three lanes as a starting portfolio, never as a quota that justifies weak matches. The job-hunter skill remains responsible for the exact location, compensation, seniority, evidence, and fit gates.
