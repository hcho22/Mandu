## Notes / Learnings

- **The HTTP 403 blockade that degraded the 07-22 run is gone.** Direct ATS reads all returned 200 today.
  That single fact is why this scan could enforce Gate 2 at all - the 07-22 run physically could not verify
  comp or remote status, which is part of why its output was unactionable.
- **Gate 2 earned its keep immediately.** Baseten's *Applied AI Inference Engineer* and *AI Solutions
  Engineer* both report `isRemote: true` while `workplaceType` reads **Hybrid, San Francisco**. Under the old
  aggregator-based method both would have been listed as remote Fit 4-5. Reading `workplaceType` instead of
  the `isRemote` boolean makes that trap structurally impossible to repeat.
- **Python's SSL trust store is broken in this environment** (`CERTIFICATE_VERIFY_FAILED`) while `curl`
  works fine. Fetch with curl, parse with Python. Worth remembering for any future scan tooling.
- **The Deepgram top pick is qualitatively different from every prior "strong fit."** Its stated bar is
  *"5+ years of professional software **or QA** engineering experience, with a track record of shipping test
  infrastructure or evaluation systems."* Every previous top-ranked role required hcho to argue that a
  hardware-QA decade should count as something else. This one asks for the thing he actually has. Its
  nice-to-have list independently names LLM/RAG/agent evaluation (Purvia), React Native (CreativeBridge),
  eval frameworks used by external teams (the starter kit), and open-source involvement.
- **Structural finding, and the most valuable output of the day:** the remote-US AI-*builder* market at these
  companies is much thinner than posting counts imply, and the pattern is systematic - **these companies hire
  engineers onsite in SF/NYC and hire revenue remotely.** Sierra's only 7 remote-US roles are all sales
  ($295K-$400K). Cursor's 19 remote roles are nearly all Account Executives. Ramp's 9 are Solutions
  Consultants. LangChain's 5 are Solutions Architects and an AE. Deepgram is the standout exception (52
  remote-US engineering postings), which is why it supplied both today's top pick and several prior leads.
- **Therefore the 5/week application target is not reachable from weekly ATS sweeps of the current company
  list under the tightened gates.** Today's honest ceiling was 3 from 58 fully-verified boards. That is a
  list-composition problem, not a scan-quality problem.
