# Machine Learning Engineer (AI Agents)

## What it is
A production ML engineer who takes AI agents from research into scaled, reliable production.
Closer to the model/infra layer than [[applied-ai-engineer]]: develops and deploys proprietary LLMs, works with training frameworks, and owns scaling, evaluation, and reliability.
Sits in the **AI builder** bucket, tilted toward ML-engineering depth rather than app-layer orchestration.

## Day-to-day
- Design, develop, deploy AI-agent solutions and proprietary models.
- Improve reasoning, planning, and evaluation in real-world scenarios.
- Scale AI systems for production; ensure performance and reliability.
- Improve security, cost-efficiency, and reliability of AI systems.

## Required qualifications and skills

Must-have:
- Bachelor's or Master's in CS/Math/related.
- 2+ years hands-on AI/ML, ideally LLMs in large-scale production.
- ML/NLP/GenAI depth; modern frameworks: PyTorch, TensorFlow, Hugging Face, NumPy.
- [[distributed-systems]] - distributed systems and cloud-based AI infrastructure.
- [[eval-pipeline-design]] - evaluation and reliability are named focus areas.
