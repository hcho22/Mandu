# Founding AI Engineer / Member of Technical Staff

## What it is
An early-hire, backend-depth engineer at an AI startup who applies AI in the real world across the whole stack.
Distinguished from [[applied-ai-engineer]] by its backend/distributed-systems weighting and founding-level ownership and equity.
Sits in the **AI builder** bucket with a startup-generalist and infrastructure tilt.

## Day-to-day
- Build resilient, secure distributed systems.
- Deploy LLMs, VLMs, and other ML in real-world settings; build agentic experiences.
- Design RAG pipelines for unstructured data and handle structured data (query planning, semantic modeling).
- Keep up with fast-moving models and infra; run toward ambiguity.

## Required qualifications and skills

Must-have:
- [[distributed-systems]] - backend depth; resilient/secure systems; comfort across languages (Python, React) and infra providers.
- [[llm-application-development]] - deploying LLMs/VLMs/ML in real-world settings.
- [[rag-systems]] - RAG for unstructured plus structured-data reasoning (query planning, semantic modeling).
- [[agent-orchestration]] - agentic experiences (bonus but valued).
- [[python]]; owner-builder mindset; comfort with ambiguity.

## Seniority and comp
Founding / senior. [[closure]]: $165K-$250K + meaningful founding-level equity. NYC or Bay Area. Note: CJIS / FBI background clearance for the criminal-justice domain (watch-out).
