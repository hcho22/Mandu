# Technical writing and benchmarking

## What it is
Producing published technical content grounded in real engineering: building benchmarks, running experiments, and writing them up at high velocity (blog pace, not paper pace).
Fuses [[eval-pipeline-design]] (the benchmark) with a public-writing surface.

## Why roles want it
The defining skill of the [[ai-content-engineer]] role (LlamaIndex): "write real code, build real benchmarks, run real experiments, then translate into published content."
Also a latent asset for any build-in-public builder role and for hcho's own product marketing.

## How to acquire or demonstrate
Show a visible portfolio of technical writing and at least one benchmark writeup.
Cadence matters: the role wants weekly output.

## hcho's current level: learning

Evidence against: the published-writing portfolio is early (a few posts), and there is no benchmark-style teardown yet.

1. Publish 1-2 benchmark teardowns from existing work (a retrieval-eval or permission-RAG benchmark writeup) - directly the deliverable this role wants, and reusable as Agentic RAG marketing.
2. Treat the build-in-public content as the writing portfolio and raise cadence.
This skill compounds with hcho's product bet, so it is dual-purpose effort.
