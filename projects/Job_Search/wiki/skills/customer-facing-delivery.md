# Customer-facing delivery

## What it is
Embedding with enterprise customers to deliver technical outcomes: running discovery, translating ambiguous business problems into shipped solutions, presenting to stakeholders, building trust, owning the technical win in pre-sales, and traveling to customer sites.
The defining, non-negotiable skill of the [[forward-deployed-engineer]] role and a heavy factor in [[applied-ai-engineer]].

## Why roles want it
FDE and enterprise Applied AI roles are built around it. Recurring phrases: "high agency," "navigate ambiguity in complex organizations," "conduct discovery," "own the technical win," "build trust with developer audiences," "25-40% travel."
It appeared in all 8 FDE postings and both Applied AI postings.

## How to acquire or demonstrate
Show evidence of customer/stakeholder-facing technical delivery: discovery, translating needs to specs, presenting, owning outcomes.
Adjacent evidence counts: consulting, solutions engineering, technical-founder customer work, or reframed cross-functional/vendor-facing experience.
