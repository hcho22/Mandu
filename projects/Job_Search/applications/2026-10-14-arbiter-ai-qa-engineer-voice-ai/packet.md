Guard tests now stop an eval from publishing a score when it measured nothing. A wrong number on a dashboard does more damage than a missing one.

I'd bring the same habits to your campaigns: test scripts that cover escalation paths and handoffs before launch, regression suites and release checks on every change, and bug reports with severity and evidence attached.
