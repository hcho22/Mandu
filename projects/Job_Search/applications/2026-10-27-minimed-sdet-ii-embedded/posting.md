About the Role

This is a Software Development Engineer in Test II - Embedded Software/Firmware role!!!

MiniMed is looking to hire an SDET II to join one of our software team in Northridge, CA. This is a hybrid role: 3-4 days in office / 1-2 days remote. Candidate will collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams to deliver end-to-end tests for embedded systems and medical devices. Software Development Engineer in Test II - Embedded Software/FirmwareThis is a Software Development Engineer in Test II - Embedded Software/Firmware role.

Responsibilities may include the following and other duties may be assigned.
- Design, develop and execute functional, regression, and end-to-end tests for embedded systems and medical devices and software components.
- Prepare test plans, test cases, test reports, defect documentation, and traceability.
- Work on scripting to automate embedded systems components using Javascript/Python.
- Understand systems software design requirements.
- Understand Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) communication testing, including pairing, connectivity, synchronization, recovery, failover, and interoperability scenarios.
- Analyze logs, processing failures to identify root causes in the embedded devices.
- May work across a variety of software lifecycle activities as determined by program
- Familiarity with AI concepts is sufficient to evaluate and collaborate with AI development teams.

Must have experience in the following areas:
- Programming: JavaScript and/or Python, used to build and maintain automated tests.
- Embedded systems: hands-on experience with embedded systems and testing embedded firmware.
- Functional testing: end-to-end functional and integration testing using HIL and SIL test benches.
- CI/CD: experience with Git and CI/CD tools such as GitLab and Jenkins. *AI tools: familiarity with AI tools for test design, scripting, or log analysis. Debugging: strong debugging skills across hardware and software.
- Nice to have: Experience with medical devices or other safety-critical systems, and knowledge of communication protocols (BLE communication, security protocols).

Required Skills & Experience
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer/Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
- 0-2 years of software engineering or test automation experience (internships and co-ops count).
- Hands-on experience with embedded systems.
- Programming experience in JavaScript and/or Python.
- Experience with end-to-end functional testing and test automation.
- Familiarity with source control and CI/CD tools (e.g., Git, GitLab, Jenkins).
- Strong debugging and problem-solving skills across hardware and software layers.
