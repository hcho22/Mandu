- **A real mechanic defect surfaced by the red team (R13):** a weekly-bucket score with `min(1, done/goal)` makes misses costless for over-goal users, contradicting "miss a day, your score dents." The shipping Score must use per-day credit (as the run-1 prototype does). **PRD US-H01 amendment recommended** - this feeds back into the iOS build.
- The claims-lint gate is a keeper pattern: the voice contract became machine-checkable and caught real violations mid-run. Candidate interview talking point alongside the Epic F/G patterns (validation rigor applied to marketing claims).
- Retention mechanic is now framed as an instrumented hypothesis with gates V1-V3 (first-session drop-off, channel demand, D7 by mechanic exposure) and a pre-committed fallback (streak-with-repair A/B).
