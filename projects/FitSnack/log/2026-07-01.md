## Notes / Learnings
- **Positioning is validated by strangers, not just the PRD.** A live r/xxfitness thread asks whether anyone "started so small as to only do ONE exercise" to build the habit - FitSnack's thesis stated in the wild.
- **Sharpest tension for the MVP:** retention benchmarks say social/accountability features cut churn 20-35% and wearable-linked apps retain better, yet the MVP deliberately ships no social/leaderboards and HealthKit-write only. The forgiving Consistency Score + identity framing have to carry retention weight two other levers usually share. Worth watching post-launch.
- **Naming/discoverability:** "exercise snacking" is media/research vocabulary, not organic search language - lead social copy with discipline/consistency, not the trend term.
