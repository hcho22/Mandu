# FitSnack - 1-Week Social Launch Plan (IG / X / TikTok)

_Created 2026-07-01. Wedge: "discipline over content." Voice: confident, concrete, no fluff, no emojis, no em dashes; FitSnack brand tone is calm, forgiving, identity-framed, anti-hype._

## The honest framing

FitSnack has no shippable app yet - the engine is mid-build (through US-C07), there's no UI, no TestFlight.
So a "download now" launch would drive people to nothing.
**This week is a build-in-public + positioning + waitlist play, not a product launch.**
The goal is to plant the "discipline over content" flag, find out if the wedge resonates with real strangers, and capture an early-access list to activate when the MVP ships.
This is the validation-first move the priorities call for, and it doubles as AI-builder portfolio content for the job search.

Success this week is measured in **waitlist signups, save/share rate, and "this is me" comments** - not views.

## The one mechanic everything drives to

Stand up a one-field early-access page on Day 0 (Tally, Carrd, or ConvertKit - ~20 minutes).
Every post ends with the same CTA: _"Early access - link in bio."_
Without this, the week produces vanity metrics and no PMF signal.
Tag links per platform (`?src=tiktok`, `?src=x`, `?src=ig`) so signups are attributable.

## Content pillars (rotate all week)

| Pillar | The line it sells | Why it works (from the research) |
|---|---|---|
| The Enemy | All-or-nothing quitting, streak anxiety, choice paralysis | 80% quit in 30 days; lost motivation = 38% of churn |
| The Promise | "5 minutes counts. No browsing, no choosing, no thinking." | The category is commoditized on content; nobody leads on removing the decision |
| The Identity | "You're someone who moves." Consistency, not streaks. | People reach for the smallest repeatable thing ("just show up") |
| Build in public | What I'm building and the opinion behind it | Feeds the builder portfolio; earns trust pre-launch |
| Borrowed proof | The science + the crowd already saying it | 2026 trend with mainstream + research backing |

## Platform roles

- **X** - the POV/manifesto engine. Text-first hot takes and build-in-public. Reaches indie + fitness + builder crowds and gets the wedge debated. Cadence: 1-2 posts/day, one thread mid-week.
- **TikTok** - the demand engine. Highest reach (this trend has 122M+ views). "Start absurdly small" demos and all-or-nothing POV skits. Cadence: 1 video/day.
- **Instagram** - home base. Reels mirror TikTok; carousels hold the manifesto (built to be saved and shared); Stories carry the build. Cadence: 1 Reel/day, 2-3 carousels across the week, daily Stories.

Produce once, repurpose thrice: each day's TikTok becomes the IG Reel; each X thread becomes an IG carousel.

## The 7-day plan

### Day 1 - Name the enemy
Big idea: open by attacking the all-or-nothing mindset, not by pitching an app.

- **X:** "Most people don't quit working out because it's hard. They quit because they missed one day, felt like a failure, and decided they'd 'restart Monday.' The streak was the problem, not you. Building something that can't be broken by one missed day."
- **TikTok/Reel (15s):** Text-on-screen over closing three fitness apps. "Deleted 3 workout apps this year. Not because they were bad. Because they made me feel like garbage the first day I missed. So I'm building the opposite."
- **IG carousel (5 slides):** "Why you keep quitting workout apps." Slide 5: the FitSnack promise + early-access CTA.

### Day 2 - The absurdly small promise
Big idea: "5 minutes counts as a full show-up." Sharpest, most counterintuitive hook.

- **X:** "Unpopular fitness take: a 5-minute workout should count exactly as much as a 45-minute one. Not for your body - for your identity. The person who shows up for 5 minutes is the person who keeps showing up. That's the whole game."
- **TikTok/Reel:** Show the core loop mockup (Figma/paper is fine pre-UI). "Open. Say how many minutes you have. Press play. No scrolling, no picking, no thinking. 5 minutes still counts."
- **IG Story poll:** "How many workouts have you abandoned this year? 0-1 / 2-3 / 4+." Free demand-research.

### Day 3 - Build in public
Big idea: show the machine and the opinion behind it. The portfolio day.

- **X thread (4-5 posts):** "I'm building a workout app with no streaks, no badges, no XP, and no AI in the core loop. Here's why every one of those is a deliberate deletion." Walk through: streaks punish, badges are noise, the engine is deterministic so it's instant and offline, the paywall is on depth not the core loop.
- **TikTok/Reel:** "Things I refuse to put in my fitness app," fast cuts: no leaderboards, no guilt notifications, no 100-day streak you can shatter.
- **IG:** Repurpose the X thread as a carousel.

### Day 4 - Borrowed proof
Big idea: not a lone crank - the science and the crowd agree.

- **X:** "'Exercise snacking' - 5 minutes a few times a day - is one of 2026's biggest wellness trends, and the research says it works for exactly the people who've given up. The gap: every app still makes you choose what to do. That's the part I'm killing."
- **TikTok/Reel:** Screen-record a Reddit thread where someone asks "has anyone started so small as to do ONE exercise just to build the habit?" then react: "This person just described the entire app I'm building."
- **IG carousel:** "The science of showing up" - 3 stats, 1 promise, 1 CTA.

### Day 5 - The identity flip
Big idea: sell who they become, not what they'll look like. Pre-empts the "does it actually work?" skeptic.

- **X:** "Your fitness app is measuring the wrong thing. Not calories. Not streaks. Whether you're becoming someone who moves. I'm building a Consistency Score that dents when you miss a week and never zeroes - because that's how identity actually works."
- **TikTok/Reel:** POV skit - "the app that shames you" vs "the app that says: you showed up, that's the win."
- **IG Story:** Behind-the-scenes of the Consistency Score logic. Humanize the build.

### Day 6 - Objection / the anti-gamification stance
Big idea: address the sharpest doubt head-on and turn it into a values statement.

- **X:** "People ask why I won't add streaks and leaderboards when the data says they boost retention. Because retention built on anxiety is a trap. I'd rather have fewer users who feel good than more users I guilt into staying. Betting the whole app on it."
- **TikTok/Reel:** "Why I said no to the feature that would've grown my app faster."
- **IG:** Carousel version, ending with the waitlist CTA framed as "join the people who are tired of being guilted."

### Day 7 - The ask + recap
Big idea: convert a week of POV into signups. Make the invitation explicit.

- **X:** Quote-tweet/recap the best post of the week: "Spent this week arguing that discipline beats content in fitness apps. The response told me I'm not the only one exhausted by streak anxiety. If you want in early: [link]. Building this in the open."
- **TikTok/Reel:** "One week of building a fitness app that refuses to guilt you. Here's what I learned about why we all quit." Soft, human close.
- **IG:** Carousel recap of the week's 5 best lines + the CTA. Pin it.

## What to measure

1. **Waitlist signups per platform** - the only real PMF signal this week. Use per-platform tagged links.
2. **Save + share rate on carousels** - a share means the message resonated enough to represent them. Higher signal than likes.
3. **"This is me" comments** - screenshot every one; validation now, testimonial copy later.
4. **Which hook converted** - double down next week on whichever line drove signups.

## Notes

- **Hashtags** are the one platform concession (discovery infrastructure, not fluff): a tight set like `#homeworkout #exercisesnacks #fitnessmotivation #buildinpublic #shorts`.
- **Emojis** are left out per hcho's style rules. Consumer fitness TikTok/IG do lean on them; add a restrained set if brand testing shows it helps reach.
- **Volume is solo-founder realistic:** ~1 video/day, 1-2 X posts/day, 2-3 IG carousels for the week. If too much, cut TikTok to 3 videos (Days 1, 2, 4) and keep X daily - X is the cheapest, highest-fit channel.
