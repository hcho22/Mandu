# Positioning Decision (run-1)

Decided 2026-07-09 by tournament: four independent contenders pitched competing angles, three independent judges (conversion marketer, target user, brand strategist) scored them.
Full pitches and verdicts in the workflow journal; scores below.
Evidence base: `../thesis/thesis.md` and `../research/*.md` (every proof point URL-grounded).

## The winner: "It's Already Ready" - the Ready Screen is the product

Unanimous, and not close:

| Pitch | Conversion judge | User judge | Strategist judge |
|---|---|---|---|
| **It's Already Ready** | **9** | **9** | **8.5** |
| The Unbreakable Start (anti-streak relief) | 6.5 | 7.5 | 5 |
| Become Someone Who Moves (identity) | 4.5 | 4 | 7 |
| The Hour You Don't Have (time scarcity) | 6 | 5 | 3.5 |

## The positioning statement

> For desk-bound adults who come home too drained to plan one more thing and who delete fitness apps mid-onboarding, this is the micro-workout app where the first screen is a complete workout, already assembled, one tap from starting - unlike Seven, Wakeout, Fitbod, and the free libraries, which make you answer questionnaires, pick vibes, browse, and filter before you are allowed to move.

## Hero copy (brand voice: calm, concrete, anti-hype)

- Primary headline: **"Open the app. Your workout is already there."**
- Alternates: "The first screen is the workout." / "It was ready before you opened it." / "Zero questions. One tap. You're moving."
- Subhead: "A complete zero-equipment session, 5 to 60 minutes, assembled on your phone before you arrive - offline, in under a tenth of a second. Between sessions it quietly retunes to how you're actually doing. Miss a day and your score dents; it never zeroes. Five minutes counts as a full show-up."

## Why it won (judges' converged reasoning)

1. **Zero promise-to-proof gap.** The hero headline, the first App Store screenshot, the first TikTok frame, and the first second of real use are the same image: a complete workout with one Start button. A cold, skeptical stranger can verify the claim before extending any trust.
2. **It attacks the measured kill zone.** Category activation collapses from 26% on day 1 to 10% by day 28; D30 install retention is ~3% (Business of Apps). The first session is where the category dies, and questionnaires/browsing sit exactly there. Forgiveness saves week four; the Ready Screen saves minute one.
3. **Defended by mechanics and by competitors' own economics.** Fitbod, Ladder, SHRED, and Alpha Progression already run anti-guesswork copy yet still open onto setup - because their questionnaires, equipment config, and content browsing ARE their conversion funnels and content moats. Copying the Ready Screen means cannibalizing themselves. That is stronger five-year defensibility than any tone-based position.
4. **It walks into empirically open ground.** The other three angles walk into owned positions (Gentler Streak owns forgiveness-led, Seven owns "just 7 minutes," identity is abstract and converts worse in a screenshot).

## What the winner absorbs from the losers (message architecture)

- **Beat 2, everywhere:** "A score that dents. Never zeroes." (from The Unbreakable Start). It answers the objection the Ready Screen creates: "fine, I'll start, but I always quit." App Store screenshot #2, second beat of every ad.
- **Win-back / retargeting track:** the full anti-streak angle for streak-burned channels. Comeback line, verbatim: "You did not lose the habit. The counter did." Plus: "Keep your chain if you love it; it just can't hurt you here."
- **Identity as the destination, not the door:** "It's already ready" is the door; "become someone who moves" is where the door leads. Identity framing lives in retention surfaces, premium-depth copy, and the eventual Strength Phase story ("someone who moves" matures into "someone who trains"). Finch (724k ratings) and Atoms ($10/mo) prove identity psychology carries premium pricing; convenience alone under-sells the $7.99/mo depth tier.
- **Time scarcity as proof, not lead:** the desk-body facts (8-hour sitting, WHO 31% inactivity, BJSM micro-dose evidence) become supporting proof points.

## Honest weaknesses carried forward (kept visible for the red team)

1. Direct demand evidence for decision-friction-at-open is thinner than the streak-anxiety evidence (one strong 2026 HN quote + analysis-paralysis posts; the causal link from open-friction to churn is INFERENCE, not a measured stat).
2. A real cohort likes browsing (NTC reviews praise choice; NTC is free). This positioning concedes the browser persona deliberately.
3. Single-headline imitation risk: "pick duration and go" already exists (Streaks Workout); the moat is the compound (ready + forgiving + adaptive), which the subhead must carry.
4. Does not answer boredom or "free alternatives" churn drivers; those are carried by the engine's variety mechanics and the free-forever core loop.
5. Post-verification correction (see `../thesis/verification.md`): "ready on open + no streaks + adaptive" is NOT unique in the broad market - Dr. Muscle and BodBot combine those for committed lifters. The positioning survives because it targets a different person (the lapsed desk-bound non-exerciser) with a different open (zero daily questions, micro-first) and a designed consistency mechanic those apps lack - but copy must never claim "the only app that..." on the broad triple.
