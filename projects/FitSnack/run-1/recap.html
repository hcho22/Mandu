Nonzero, in five minutes.

A micro-workout iOS app for desk-bound adults who quit fitness apps. Open it and a complete 5 to 60 minute workout is already there. Miss a day and your Consistency Score dents; it never zeroes. This page is the map to everything run-1 produced: the evidence, the name, the brand, the site, the videos, and the red team that tried to kill it.

Built 2026-07-08/09, entirely locally, from the FitSnack v6 PRD. Working name FitSnack was rebranded Nonzero on availability evidence. Open this file from projects/FitSnack/run-1/ so the relative links work.

1. The business, in one verdict

The thesis that survived: busy desk-bound adults abandon fitness apps at measured, catastrophic rates (~3% of installs remain at day 30). Two of the mechanisms behind that are design choices, not laws: apps demand decisions at open, and they zero your progress when you miss. Nonzero deletes both: the first screen is a complete, adaptive, zero-equipment workout, and the score dents but never zeroes. No shipping app serves that compound to this user.

What the evidence killed along the way: the folklore stats ("80% quit in 30 days"), the "forgiving fitness" positioning (owned by Gentler Streak), the longevity claim (vigorous-only evidence), broad uniqueness ("the only app that..." is false: Dr. Muscle, BodBot), and the comfortable income math (honest bar: 10-20k installs/month; see the red team).

Full argument with sources: the thesis (every claim URL-grounded, counter-evidence ledger kept visible), audited by 12 adversarial verifiers (9 confirmed, 2 weakened, 1 refuted and corrected). The raw research: pain voices, pain stats, micro-workout competitors, incumbents, the open wedge, market and channels, the science, retention evidence, pricing evidence, discovery demand.

6. The red team, visible

Six skeptics attacked pain, moat, retention, pricing, name, and GTM; a completeness critic swept the package. 38 objections, every one dispositioned: objections and dispositions (raw attacks). The three that most deserve a stranger's attention:

  • The income math: on the package's own benchmarks, income-relevance needs 10-20k installs/month sustained, more than every named entrant in the niche except the declining leader. Until the channel test clears its thresholds, this is a portfolio asset with revenue upside.
  • The retention mechanic is a hypothesis: no direct evidence a dent-score retains anyone; every measured forgiveness win is bolted onto an intact streak. Gates V1-V3 in the plan instrument it and pre-commit the fallback.
  • A real code defect: as first coded, an over-goal user's miss cost zero, contradicting "miss a day, your score dents." Reproduced, dispositioned, and turned into a mechanic-copy contract (the shipping score must use the prototype's per-day credit model).
