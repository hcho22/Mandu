# Marketing Engineer

The person who turns market signal into pipeline using AI agents, data, code, and taste.
From [[projects/Education/Marketing/wiki/sources/becoming-a-marketing-engineer|Becoming a Marketing Engineer]], where [[projects/Education/AI/AI_Wiki/wiki/entities/greg-isenberg|Greg Isenberg]] predicts it becomes one of the most valuable roles in tech over the next 18 to 24 months; he also calls it the forward deployed marketer or the AI growth operator, and expects the name to change while the job stays.

## What changed to create the role

The speaker's four eras, each defined by what the best marketer did:

| Era | The best marketer |
|---|---|
| Traditional ("Don Draper") | Made people care through story and psychology, on the channels of the day |
| Digital (from about 2005-06, his estimate) | Acquired customers through measurable channels: search, ads, landing pages, funnels |
| Growth hacking (about 2008-2011, hedged) | Used the product itself as the growth engine: activation, referral, onboarding, retention, pricing |
| Marketing engineering (now) | Builds a marketing system that keeps learning, from AI, agents, data, code, and taste |

The old skills stay; the new part is building the system behind the marketing.
The problem the system solves is scattered learning: sales, support, product, marketing, and the founder each hear a different version of the market.

## The systems it builds

| System | What it does | Related page |
|---|---|---|
| Customer truth system | A daily or weekly file of what the market is saying and what changed, with receipts | [[customer-truth-file]] |
| Founder content engine | One insight into many surfaces; performance decides what to repeat | [[content-source-library]] |
| Outbound signal engine | Outreach triggered by timing signals, drafted by an agent, approved by a human | - |
| Creative testing engine | One offer into 20 hooks and 10 angles, run as a learning system | [[projects/Education/AI/AI_Wiki/wiki/concepts/test-breadth-over-conviction|Test breadth over conviction]] |
| AI search visibility | Be understood and cited by AI answer engines | [[projects/Education/AI/AI_Wiki/wiki/concepts/aeo-answer-engine-optimization|AEO]] |
| Growth cockpit | A weekly memo on what changed and what to test next | [[projects/Education/AI/AI_Wiki/wiki/concepts/production-watchdog|Production watchdog]] |

All of them sit on a [[growth-repo]], and every agent in them gets a job spec: data source, schedule, filters, expected output, approval points, metric, and where results are written.

## Key points

- **Measure results, not activity.** Qualified replies, booked demos, and conversion are the signal; messages sent and posts published are not.
- **A human approves anything that goes out.** Outbound and content drafts go to a person before they are sent.
- **Start narrow.** One working system beats five half-built ones, and the tighter the wedge, the easier it is to sell, deliver, and repeat.
- **Agents become a commodity; judgment about what to point them at is the moat.**

## Synthesis

Read through AI_Wiki's definitions, the role is the human who builds what [[projects/Education/AI/AI_Wiki/wiki/concepts/marketing-agent|Marketing agent]] describes: a defined process on a cadence, over data, with a feedback loop, plus the context layer that keeps it on-message.
AI_Wiki recorded that union as undocumented on 2026-08-21; this source describes it, with a hypothetical company and no working system, so it narrows the gap on paper only.

The discipline underneath is validation discipline pointed at marketing.
Every system carries an evidence rule (quotes, links, counts), a defined metric, and a human sign-off, and the 30-day plan ends in a measured case study.
For hcho that is the transferable part; the pay forecasts are one speaker's predictions and support nothing on their own.

The source compares the role to the [[projects/Education/AI/AI_Wiki/wiki/concepts/forward-deployed-engineer|forward deployed engineer]]: an embedded builder paid for outcomes next to revenue.
hcho's own [[projects/gr0wthlab/README|gr0wthlab]] carries the "Marketing Engineer" name for a narrower, stricter thing: an agent that drafts and proposes while hcho decides and nothing is sent.
