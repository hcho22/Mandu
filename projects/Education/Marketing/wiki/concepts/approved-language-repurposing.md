# Approved Language Repurposing

Adapt an author's currently endorsed material into new formats while preserving its meaning, attribution, and factual limits.
[[projects/Education/Marketing/wiki/sources/build-a-digital-brain-guide|The digital-brain guide]] assigns AI the retrieval and adaptation work while leaving original thinking and endorsement with the author.

## Key points

- Supply the relevant passages, intended audience, format, and concrete voice preferences.
- Trace personal and factual claims to source passages; missing support should remain visible rather than being filled with invention.
- A retrieved sentence is not necessarily a current belief: identify superseded positions and conflicting versions.
- Review recombinations, because two individually supported passages can be joined into an unsupported conclusion.
- Attribute a founder's or employee's experience to that person rather than turning it into a brand's autobiography.
- A generated variation becomes approved language only after the author approves it.

## Synthesis

[[projects/Education/AI/AI_Wiki/wiki/concepts/personal-voice-skill|Personal voice skills]] address style; this concept adds the separate question of whether the author endorses the claim.
Examples and explicit vocabulary or rhythm choices are complementary, despite different emphasis in the two guides.
Mandu can automatically file a third-party tutorial without thereby treating its claims as hcho's beliefs or granting publication approval.
The source-only boundary for first-person claims is compatible with supplementing learning answers using clearly attributed outside evidence.
