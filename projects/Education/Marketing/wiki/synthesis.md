# Marketing Synthesis

With one derived source, this library has a working hypothesis rather than a corroborated marketing strategy: useful AI-assisted content depends on specific, attributable human inputs.
[[content-source-library]] captures those inputs, [[approved-language-repurposing]] bounds adaptation, and [[content-value-and-time-horizon]] separates knowledge contribution from distribution performance.

The key boundary for Mandu is between learning a third party's idea and endorsing it as hcho's own position.
Automatic ingestion can support the former; it cannot infer the latter.
Likewise, the guide's focused publishing-topic advice is compatible with a broad learning library because audience positioning and knowledge collection serve different purposes.
