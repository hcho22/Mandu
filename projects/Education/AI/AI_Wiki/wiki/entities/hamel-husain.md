# Hamel Husain

Independent ML consultant and a prominent voice on LLM evaluation.
Five years building language-model products, starting with CodeSearchNet at GitHub, now helping companies build domain-specific AI products.

## What he argues

- Systematic evaluation, not prompt-tweaking, is what separates real AI products from demos (see [[your-ai-product-needs-evals]]).
- Favors simple, problem-specific eval systems over generic frameworks and expensive tooling.
- Emphasizes [[looking-at-your-data]] as the non-negotiable habit, and validating [[llm-as-judge]] against human labels.
