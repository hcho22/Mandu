# Cody Schneider

Marketer and builder working on agent-driven growth systems; associated with **companiesgraph.com**.
The wiki's primary source on agents pointed at distribution rather than at code.

Background: a partner in a WordPress migration agency around 2011, officially partnered with WordPress to migrate large sites (including Time.com and TechCrunch.com) off custom CMSs.

## What shows up in the wiki

From [[building-marketing-agents]]:

- [[marketing-agent]] - his three-requirement definition (data clarity, autonomous decision loop, infrastructure), offered against what he says is loose usage of the term.
- [[unified-data-warehouse-for-agents]] - Airbyte plus ClickHouse; the infrastructure precondition most people skip.
- [[creative-entropy]] - **the most generalizable idea in the source**: an agent left alone gets stuck thinking the same way, and needs DNA injected from outside its own output.
- [[test-breadth-over-conviction]] - 10-20 positioning variants before concluding; "we're not Don Draper."
- [[creative-is-the-targeting]] - post-Andromeda, the algorithm reads your creative and landing page and does the audience-matching.
- [[manager-of-agents]] - his "agent jockey" framing is the marketing-side version of the same role reframe.

## Positions worth knowing

- **He explicitly does not want a fully autonomous agent.** He wants something that executes a defined process, looks at the resulting data, and improves from that feedback - a narrower, more controllable definition than the hype.
- **"An agent is just code"** - a decision tree fed a live data stream with an LLM decision loop optimizing toward an outcome.
- **The agent isn't why ad accounts get banned.** Bans come from ToS violations, typically spamming the API for bulk data. Use the API for write actions.
- **Marketing is continuous, not campaign-based**, because feedback loops are now much faster.
- On Reddit as a research source, he is candid about the problem he contributes to: threads are increasingly manipulated so LLMs will cite them - "the dead internet" - and "I am the problem."
