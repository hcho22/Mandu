# Josh Pigford

Serial solo founder (sold Baremetrics for $4M); builds ~5 AI products in parallel with agents.
X/Twitter @Spigford; blog everydayisayear.ai.

## What shows up in the wiki

- [[building-5-ai-products-solo]] - his AI-assisted workflow: [[phased-implementation]] in isolated worktrees, [[adversarial-code-review]] (Opus builds, GPT-5.5 reviews) plus a "but-for-real" self-critique, [[self-updating-config]], brand-first design, and [[ship-fast-learn-from-users]].
- Maintains ~a dozen open-source skills (build, research, learnings, but-for-real, ui.sh, in-app support).

A useful reference model for hcho's solo-builder path, with one instructive difference: Pigford ships the whole product and relies on 25 years of judgment rather than up-front demand validation - the counterpoint to hcho's validation-first Agentic RAG plan.
