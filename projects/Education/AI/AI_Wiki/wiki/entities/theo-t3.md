# Theo (T3 / soydev)

Developer, educator, and YouTuber; builder of **T3 Code**, an open-source tool for remote-controlling coding agents across machines (web + desktop + mobile).

## What shows up in the wiki

- [[working-with-fable-5]] - his workflow for [[anthropic|Anthropic's]] Fable 5 in [[claude-code]]: [[reasoning-effort-discipline]], [[model-routing]] (shell out to GPT-5.5 via Codex), a [[model-selection]] glossary in `CLAUDE.md`, and a live run that cleared a 16-PR backlog and shipped a month of work in ~5.5 hours for ~$150.
- Source of [[time-to-fix-as-health-signal]] and a concrete staging-only auto-merge safety pattern.
- [[token-maxing-with-agents]] - the throughput companion to the cost video: [[subscription-vs-api-tokens]] (flat-rate plans as the funding model), [[delegate-the-problem-not-the-solution]], [[agent-threads-as-inbox]] in [[t3-code]], an owned always-on Linux box for overnight runs, and a multi-account proxy he himself flags as a terms-of-service risk.

## Notes

Practitioner voice, not a vendor: his model ratings and reasoning-effort claims are experience-based reads of the current Claude 5 family, useful as working defaults rather than benchmarks.
Reports spending "over $250k" building T3 Code (open source, un-monetized).
The later video says the "300k+" spend figures people cite are not what he actually pays; the two figures are not reconciled in the sources and may be token value rather than cash.
T3 Connect now exists with a free three-device tier ([[t3-code]]).

**Same speaker, two emphases (2026-10-02).** The Fable 5 video is about spending less per run ([[reasoning-effort-discipline]], [[model-routing]]); the token-maxing video is about using more of a flat allowance.
They are compatible because both assume subscriptions: one optimizes efficiency per run, the other utilization per reset window ([[subscription-vs-api-tokens]]).
His model ratings move with releases: Opus 4.8 rated high on taste in the first video, Opus called "a trash model" in the second, then Opus 5.5 called "pretty good" in a later inserted clip ([[model-selection]]).
