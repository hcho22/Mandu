# Product Wrapper

The layer around an agent that turns a cool automation into a real product, from [[agents-are-the-new-saas]]: "the agent does the work, but the wrapper creates the trust."

## What the wrapper provides

- **Visibility:** logs and a record of what happened and why the agent did it.
- **Control:** approvals, controls, and handoff rules.
- **Safety to adopt:** a way to test the agent before it goes live.

The agent itself lives in the phone system, inbox, Slack channel, or CRM, but the customer still needs a "control room" dashboard (which can be simple): call summaries, reservation outcomes, tickets created, vendor routes, owner approvals.

## Synthesis

The wrapper is where trust is manufactured, and trust is the actual product when you are [[agent-saas-sells-work|selling work]] rather than a tool.
It is also the natural home for the eval story: the pre-launch test set (see [[evals]]) and the run logs live in the same surface. For hcho, the wrapper is the visible artifact that a validation-minded builder is unusually well-positioned to get right.
