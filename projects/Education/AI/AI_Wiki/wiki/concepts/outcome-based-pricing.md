# Outcome-Based Pricing

The pricing thesis from [[agents-are-the-new-saas]]: because [[agent-saas-sells-work]], the future is pricing on outcomes ("per qualified appointment") rather than per software seat.

## Key points

- **Sell the pilot like labor first.** Start with three customers in one niche, same workflow and pain, and sell the outcome ("we will answer and qualify your missed calls").
- **Start simple, then move to outcomes.** Example ladder: `$1,500 setup + $1,000/month` (one workflow) then `$2,000 setup + $30 per qualified appointment` then `$3,000/month up to 500 handled tickets`.
- **Earn your way to outcome pricing;** do not jump straight to it. First learn what the customer values, where the agent breaks, and what they would miss if you removed it.
- **Productize the repeated pattern:** if every business in the niche needs the same steps, that repeatable pattern is the product. "You earn the software by doing the work first."

## Synthesis

Outcome pricing aligns price with the value actually delivered, which is only credible if you can measure the outcome - another reason [[evals]] and the [[product-wrapper]] logs matter commercially.
For hcho's own bets, the transferable move is the pilot-then-productize sequence: do the work manually with AI for a few customers, find what repeats, and let that define the product rather than guessing the feature set up front.
