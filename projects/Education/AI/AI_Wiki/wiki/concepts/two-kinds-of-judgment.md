# The Two Kinds of Judgment

Why [[forward-deployed-engineer]]s are rare, from [[fde-in-30-days]].

| | Covers | Who has it |
|---|---|---|
| **Commercial / business judgment** | Workflows, cost, incentives, risk, adoption, business value, internal politics | Consultants - McKinsey, BCG, Bain engagement managers |
| **Technical judgment** | Models, systems, APIs, data, code reliability, evals, guardrails, harnesses, post-training, fine-tuning | Software engineers |

**An FDE must be the best combination of both - not an average, not "good enough" at either side.**

> "It is truly the best of both... that is the million-dollar hire where the FDE can turn business understanding into working software end to end."

## The warning

As FDE hiring accelerates, many people entering the role are strong in only one dimension - **good communicators who can't code, or good engineers who can't communicate.**

Vas "strongly urges" people to actively strengthen whichever side is weaker.
The framing offered in the conversation: understanding art and understanding science, and being able to speak both.

## Synthesis

The word doing the work here is **"best of both," not "average of both."**
That distinction is what makes the role scarce and well-paid, and it is also the honest bar - a hybrid profile is only valuable if each half clears a real threshold, not if it's two halves of a competent whole.

This is the most useful diagnostic frame in the batch for hcho's self-assessment, because it asks a sharper question than "am I technical enough."

On the technical side the gap is narrowing and is measurable: production LLM and agentic systems shipped, an eval harness, RLS and permissions work, a real iOS app.
The named sub-skills in the technical column - **evals, guardrails, harnesses** - are disproportionately the ones he has.

On the commercial side the honest read is thinner.
Ten years at Zebra is deep *domain and operational* judgment, which is adjacent to but not the same as the commercial column here: cost modelling, incentives, adoption, and internal politics as things you actively work rather than observe.
The Rep Today GTM package and the Agentic RAG pricing and buyer work are the first real reps at it, and they are recent and untested against a paying client.

The actionable version: the buyer interviews are not only product validation, they are **the commercial-judgment reps**, and running them as [[audit-eval-deployment]] Phase-1 engagements would build the weaker column while doing work already on the plan.
That is a better use of them than treating them purely as a product-discovery exercise.
