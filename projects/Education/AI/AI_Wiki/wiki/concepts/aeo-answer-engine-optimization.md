# AEO (Answer/Agent Engine Optimization)

From [[startups-for-agents]]: as agents (and answer engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity) decide who to cite, trust, and recommend, SEO gives way to AEO - optimizing to be chosen by machines rather than found by humans.

## The six shifts AEO implies

- **Forms -> tool calls:** the call-to-action becomes an action endpoint.
- **Support docs -> executable support:** agents perform the refund, return, reschedule, escalation.
- **Landing pages -> capability manifests:** agents care about what they can *do*, not slogans.
- **Sales calls -> agent procurement:** buyers send agents to a shortlist before a human appears.
- **Analytics -> agent analytics:** which agents visited, what they asked, where they failed or bounced.
- **SEO -> AEO:** rank in the places agents ask (other agents, computer-use tools, answer engines).

## The open question

The host cannot stop thinking about **agent analytics and the agent conversion rate**: he once lifted human conversion 1%->4% by pairing voice-of-customer data with Google Analytics, and there is no agent-native equivalent yet.

## As one of six growth systems (2026-10-14)

[[projects/Education/Marketing/wiki/sources/becoming-a-marketing-engineer|Becoming a Marketing Engineer]] (Marketing wiki) lists "AI search visibility" as one of a [[projects/Education/Marketing/wiki/concepts/marketing-engineer|marketing engineer]]'s six systems: check whether the company is understandable to AI systems, then have agents pull data, create content, and optimize the site so it gets cited.
The speaker focuses on ChatGPT over Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude, citing a billion-plus users from memory (unverified).
It offers no method beyond that outline, so it confirms the priority rather than adding technique.

## Synthesis

AEO is the go-to-market face of the [[agentic-web]], and its downstream shifts pull marketing toward engineering: a "capability manifest" is an [[agent-readable-website]], and "agent analytics" is [[agent-observability]] turned outward onto visiting agents.
The undefined agent-conversion problem is a wide-open measurement space - and measurement of agent behavior against a goal is close kin to evals, so it is a natural place for hcho's discipline to land if the area matures.
